              Not a family account?

              NGOs, rescue partners and shelter volunteers sign in on separate portals. Switching logs you out of this account first.

              NGO desk

              An NGO sees only the requests sent to it. Requests are listed in priority order, most urgent first. Accept each request and tick it off when help is delivered. Statuses show on the family’s phone.

              How the priority order is decided
              Score = need (rescue 100, medical 60, shelter 40, food 30, clothes 10, papers 5) + vulnerable household members (pregnant woman, someone ill 15; child, elderly, disability 12 each) + 2 per person (up to 10) + waiting time (up to 20). An active SOS is always Critical. Completed requests drop to the bottom.
