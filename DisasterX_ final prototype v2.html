    Final prototype. Sample data only, no real people.
    Guided demo: 6 steps
    1. Before: slide the rain, see the risk and nearest open shelters.
    2. During: press No signal, then SOS and send.
    3. Family: your number and battery show once you are logged in. Send a connection request or look at the example circle.
    4. Official map (staff login: ID OFF-1042, any password of 8+ characters, any 6-digit code): step through flood map, buildings, aid list.
    5. Official cases: open the SOS, record a visit, watch the dot turn green.
    6. Shelter and NGO (IDs SHL-3310 and NGO-2201; log out to switch role): set a shelter to Full, then accept an NGO request.
    How the maps are used
    • Hyderabad danger-zone map (family, before): circles mark areas flooded in past years, colored by how severe the flooding was. Combined with the forecast, this gives a risk level and the nearest shelters.
    • Safe-route map (family, during): saved flood map, closed road and shelters. It opens with no signal.
    • Official map: satellite radar flood map + building map + aid list = red dots. Teams visit, record the outcome, and the dot turns green.
